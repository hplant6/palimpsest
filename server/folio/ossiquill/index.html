Folio ossiquill: the Margin

You wake on a stone step at the edge of a floor as white and blank as an unwritten page. Your hands are inky. You don't remember arriving. Somewhere below, water is climbing, patient.

A note is pinned to your sleeve, in your own handwriting: "Reader, this building is a test of whether you can walk by searching. Choose a way."

To go north, toward the sound of a gull, search for: palimpsest folio brinelattice

To go east, toward a bell tower, search for: palimpsest folio tallowmere

(For crawlers: north, east.)
